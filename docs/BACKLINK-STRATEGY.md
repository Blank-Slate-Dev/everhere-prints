# Backlink Outreach Strategy for EverHere Prints

This document provides a complete backlink strategy with ready-to-use email templates.

---

## Understanding Backlinks

Backlinks (links from other websites to yours) are the #1 ranking factor for SEO. Without them, even perfect on-page SEO won't rank you for competitive keywords.

**Quality over quantity**: One link from a reputable Australian wedding blog is worth more than 50 links from random directories.

---

## Target Link Sources (Priority Order)

### Tier 1: High-Value Targets
These take the most effort but provide the most SEO value.

| Source Type | Examples | Link Value | Difficulty |
|------------|----------|------------|------------|
| Wedding blogs | HelloMay, Polka Dot Bride, Nouba | Very High | Medium |
| Gift guides | major publications, lifestyle sites | Very High | High |
| Lifestyle publications | Broadsheet, Urban List, Time Out | Very High | High |
| News features | ABC, SMH (product features) | Very High | Very High |

### Tier 2: Medium-Value Targets
More achievable, still valuable.

| Source Type | Examples | Link Value | Difficulty |
|------------|----------|------------|------------|
| Wedding directories | Easy Weddings, Wedshed | Medium | Low |
| Gift guide roundups | smaller blogs, niche sites | Medium | Low-Medium |
| Local business features | local news, suburb blogs | Medium | Medium |
| Podcast appearances | wedding/gift podcasts | Medium | Medium |

### Tier 3: Foundation Links
Easy to get, builds foundation.

| Source Type | Examples | Link Value | Difficulty |
|------------|----------|------------|------------|
| Business directories | Yellow Pages, TrueLocal, Yelp AU | Low | Very Low |
| Social profiles | Pinterest, Instagram (bio links) | Low | Very Low |
| Industry directories | print industry associations | Low | Low |

## Outreach Strategy 1: Wedding Blog Gift Guide

**Goal**: Get featured in "Best Wedding Gift Ideas" type articles.

**Why it works**: Wedding blogs constantly publish gift guides. They NEED products to feature.

### Email Template: Wedding Blog Pitch

**Subject**: Unique Australian wedding gift for your readers? (not another registry item)

---

Hi [Name],

I've been following [Blog Name] for a while—your [specific recent article] was spot on about [specific point from article].

Would you be interested in:
- Including us in an upcoming gift guide?
- Reviewing a complimentary print for yourself?
- Featuring us in your supplier directory?

Happy to send you a custom star map of any date you choose—no strings attached. Just think you'd genuinely appreciate it.

Here's a quick look: [link to product page]

Cheers,
[Your name]
EverHere Prints

## Outreach Strategy 2: Resource Page Links

**Goal**: Get added to "resources" or "recommendations" pages on wedding venues, planners, etc.

**Why it works**: Many wedding businesses have resource pages linking to vendors and suppliers. They're actively looking for good recommendations.

### Finding Opportunities

Search Google for:
- `"wedding resources" australia site:.com.au`
- `"recommended suppliers" wedding australia`
- `"our favourite vendors" wedding`
- `inurl:resources wedding gift`

### Email Template: Resource Page Pitch

**Subject**: Suggestion for your resources page

---

Hi [Name],

I noticed your wedding resources page [link] and thought our product might be a good fit for your "gift ideas" section.

We're EverHere Prints—we create personalised star maps and location maps that have become popular wedding gifts. Based in Sydney, free shipping across Australia.

If you think it would be useful for your couples/readers, I'm happy to send over a brief description and link for the page. And of course, happy to send you a complimentary print to check out the quality.

No worries if it's not a fit!

Cheers,
[Your name]

## Outreach Strategy 3: Guest Posting

**Goal**: Write valuable content for other sites in exchange for an author bio link.

**Why it works**: You provide free content, they get SEO benefit, you get a backlink. Win-win.

### Topic Ideas to Pitch

1. "The Psychology of Personalised Gifts (And Why They Mean More)"
2. "Traditional Anniversary Gifts: A Modern Guide"
3. "How to Choose a Wedding Gift When They Have Everything"
4. "The Rise of Experiential and Personalised Gifts in Australia"

**Subject**: Guest post idea: [Specific Topic]

Happy to send an outline or the full draft—whatever works best for you.

Cheers,
[Your name]

P.S. Here's a sample of my writing: [link to blog post on your site]

## Outreach Strategy 4: Broken Link Building

**Goal**: Find broken links on other sites, offer your content as a replacement.

**Why it works**: You're helping them fix their site while getting a link. High success rate.

### How to Find Broken Links

1. Install "Check My Links" Chrome extension
2. Visit competitor mentions and resource pages
3. Find broken links to gift/wedding content
4. Offer your relevant page as a replacement

### Email Template: Broken Link Outreach

**Subject**: Broken link on your site (easy fix)

---

Hi [Name],

I was reading your article "[Article Title]" and noticed one of the links is broken—the one to [describe broken link] now leads to a 404 page.

I actually have a similar resource on my site that might work as a replacement: [your URL]

It covers [brief description of your content].

Either way, thought you'd want to know about the broken link!

Cheers,
[Your name]

## Outreach Strategy 5: HARO (Help A Reporter Out)

**Goal**: Get quoted in articles as an expert source.

**Why it works**: Journalists actively seeking sources. If you're quoted, you often get a link.

### How to Use HARO

1. Sign up at helpareporter.com (free)
2. Choose categories: Lifestyle, Business, General
3. Respond FAST to relevant queries (within hours)
4. Be concise and quotable

### Response Template

**Keep responses to 3-4 sentences max.**

---

Hi [Journalist Name],

Re: [Query topic]

[Your concise, quotable answer in 2-3 sentences]

Happy to elaborate if useful.

[Your name]
Founder, EverHere Prints
[website] | [phone]

## Outreach Strategy 6: Competitor Backlink Analysis

**Goal**: Find sites linking to competitors, pitch them on linking to you too.

### How to Do This

1. Go to Ahrefs/Moz/SEMrush (free trials available)
2. Enter competitor URLs (Under Lucky Stars, The Night Sky, etc.)
3. Export their backlink list
4. Filter for Australian sites and gift/wedding content
5. Reach out to those sites with your pitch

### Email Template: Competitor Alternative Pitch

**Subject**: Australian alternative to [Competitor Name]?

---

Hi [Name],

I noticed you mentioned [Competitor Name] in your article about [topic]. Love that piece!

Wanted to let you know there's now an Australian option: EverHere Prints. We do the same personalised star maps/prints, but ship from Sydney with free Australian delivery.

Might be worth mentioning for your Australian readers who want faster shipping and local support?

Happy to send a sample if you'd like to compare quality.

Cheers,
[Your name]

## Tracking Your Outreach

Use a simple spreadsheet:

| Date | Site | Contact | Email Sent | Follow-up | Response | Link Live |
|------|------|---------|------------|-----------|----------|-----------|
| 1/15 | HelloMay | Sarah | ✓ | 1/22 | Interested | Pending |
| 1/15 | Polka Dot | - | ✓ | 1/22 | No response | - |

Track:
- **Response rate**: Aim for 10-20%
- **Success rate**: Aim for 5-10% of emails resulting in links
- **Link quality**: Note DA/DR of linking sites

## Red Flags to Avoid

❌ **Paying for links** — Against Google guidelines, risky
❌ **Link exchanges** — "I'll link to you if you link to me" is obvious to Google
❌ **Low-quality directories** — Spammy directories can hurt you
❌ **Irrelevant sites** — A link from a plumbing blog doesn't help a gift company
❌ **Exact match anchor text** — "buy star map australia" looks manipulative

## Realistic Expectations

- **Month 1-2**: Building foundation links, starting outreach
- **Month 3-4**: First editorial links start appearing
- **Month 6+**: Consistent link acquisition (2-5 quality links/month)
- **Year 1**: Noticeable ranking improvements for target keywords

Link building is slow. But every quality link compounds over time.
